The fields of software, computing, and computer science face underrepresentation by gender, race, ethnicity, geography, and family income. Jobs in computer science are expected to grow 13% by 2026, but the unfair proportion of representation may remain the same until we provide resources to all communities to expose them to computer science. And we can do so by starting within our own community: high school students. OneStopCS equipts students with information about how their interests relates to computer science and what they can do to gain computer science skills, while having fun.
